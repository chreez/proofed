## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
From the 2026-04-11 sourdough pizza bake: when cooking pizzas a few hours before an event, need a reliable transport method that protects flavor and crust texture without excessive plastic wrap or foil.

Current thinking from bake notes:
- Quarter sheets lined with parchment paper
- Layering slices in large Tupperware with parchment between layers
- Weight of stacked layers shouldn't compress bottom slices too much

Research needed:
- Best practices for transporting homemade pizza (professional and home methods)
- How long pizza holds at room temp before quality degrades
- Reheating options at the destination (if available)
- Container recommendations that balance reusability, cost, and other bake uses
