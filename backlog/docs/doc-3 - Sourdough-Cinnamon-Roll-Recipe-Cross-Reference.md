## Narrative Analysis

### 1. Starter Amounts and Leavening Strategy

The recipes split into three distinct camps:

**Pure sourdough leavening** (no yeast, no chemical leaveners):
- The Perfect Loaf, Clever Carrot, Halicopter Away
- These rely entirely on active starter/levain for rise
- Require longer fermentation (8-24 hours total)
- Starter amounts range from 100-227g

**Sourdough + chemical leavener hybrid**:
- Little Spoon Farm, Farmhouse on Boone
- Use starter primarily for flavor, with baking powder/soda providing lift
- Interesting technique: both add BP/BS AFTER the overnight ferment, not before
- This gives sourdough flavor development without relying on it for structure
- Both use overnight room-temp fermentation (10-12 hrs)

**Sourdough + commercial yeast hybrid** (optional):
- King Arthur is the ONLY recipe that explicitly includes optional commercial yeast (1 tsp instant)
- This is the most relevant model for our ATK adaptation since ATK original uses both BP and yeast

### 2. Flour: AP vs Bread Flour

**All-purpose flour**: Clever Carrot, Little Spoon Farm, Farmhouse on Boone (4 of 6)
**Bread flour**: The Perfect Loaf, Halicopter Away (2 of 6)
**Mixed**: King Arthur (AP + golden wheat)

The consensus leans AP. However, the two most enriched recipes (Perfect Loaf and Halicopter Away) both use bread flour. This makes sense: higher protein handles the extra fat and sugar better and provides more structure to compensate for the weakening effect of enrichments.

**For our ATK adaptation**: Bread flour is the right call since we're working with a highly enriched dough (butter, eggs, milk).

### 3. Butter Amounts

| Recipe | Butter in dough (g) | Baker's % of flour |
|---|---|---|
| The Perfect Loaf | 139g | 24% |
| King Arthur | 57g | 11% |
| Clever Carrot | 28g | 8% |
| Little Spoon Farm | 113g (cold, grated) | 32% |
| Farmhouse on Boone | 0g (coconut oil) | 0% |
| Halicopter Away | 60g | 18% |

Huge range: 0-139g. The Perfect Loaf is the richest (true brioche territory at 24%). Little Spoon Farm uses MORE butter by baker's % but incorporates it cold (biscuit technique), yielding a flaky rather than brioche texture.

**For our ATK adaptation**: ATK original uses 140g total butter (113g in dough + 28g brushed). The Perfect Loaf's 139g is nearly identical. This confirms we can maintain ATK's butter load with sourdough.

### 4. Egg Usage

- **3 eggs**: The Perfect Loaf (most enriched, true brioche)
- **2 eggs**: Farmhouse on Boone
- **1 egg**: King Arthur, Clever Carrot, Halicopter Away
- **0 eggs**: Little Spoon Farm

The majority use 1 egg. Only the brioche-style Perfect Loaf recipe uses 3. For our ATK adaptation (which has no eggs in the original), adding 1 egg is a safe moderate choice. Adding 2-3 would push it toward brioche territory, which may or may not be the goal.

### 5. Rise/Proof Times

**Same-day possible**:
- King Arthur: 4 hrs first rise + 2-3 hrs second rise = 6-7 hrs minimum
- The Perfect Loaf: 2 hrs warm + 2 hrs fridge + 2-3 hrs proof = 6-7 hrs minimum

**Overnight required**:
- Clever Carrot: 8-12 hrs overnight + 1-2 hrs second rise
- Little Spoon Farm: 10-12 hrs overnight, no second rise
- Farmhouse on Boone: 8-12 hrs overnight, no second rise
- Halicopter Away: 8-24 hrs cold + 6-10 hrs proof (longest total)

**Consensus**: Most sourdough cinnamon roll recipes are overnight recipes. The two that can be done same-day (King Arthur, Perfect Loaf) still require 6-7 hours minimum.

**For our ATK adaptation**: An overnight option is essential. The ATK original is a same-day 2-hour recipe. Our sourdough version will need a different timeline -- either:
- Option A: Same-day with optional yeast assist (King Arthur model, ~6-7 hrs)
- Option B: Overnight ferment, bake in morning (majority model)
- Option C: Flexible 2-day (Perfect Loaf model, fridge retard overnight)

### 6. Baking Temps and Times

| Recipe | Temp | Time | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Perfect Loaf | 400F | 20-25 min | Highest temp, shortest time |
| King Arthur | 400F | 18-25 min | Highest temp, shortest time |
| Little Spoon Farm | 375F | 25-30 min | Mid temp, tent at 15-20 min |
| Farmhouse on Boone | 375F | 20-25 min | Mid temp |
| Clever Carrot | 350F | 35-40 min | Lowest temp, longest time |
| Halicopter Away | 350F | 30-35 min | Lowest temp, long time |

**Clear pattern**: Higher enrichment = higher temp + shorter time. The two most enriched recipes (Perfect Loaf, King Arthur) both bake at 400F for 20-25 min. The less enriched ones go 350-375F for 25-40 min.

**For our ATK adaptation**: ATK original bakes at 350F for 25-30 min. Given our enriched dough, bumping to 375-400F and reducing time to 20-25 min would align with the sourdough enriched dough consensus.

### 7. Pan Types

- **9x13 rectangular**: The Perfect Loaf, King Arthur
- **Cast iron skillet (12-14")**: Little Spoon Farm, Farmhouse on Boone
- **9-inch springform**: Clever Carrot
- **Generic baking tin**: Halicopter Away

ATK original uses a 10-inch cast iron skillet. Both the 9x13 pan and cast iron skillet are well-represented. The Clever Carrot's springform recommendation is interesting -- the higher sides trap moisture for softer rolls.

### 8. Hybrid Yeast + Sourdough Recipes

**King Arthur is the only pure hybrid** (sourdough starter + optional commercial yeast). This is the closest model to what we need for the ATK adaptation.

**Little Spoon Farm and Farmhouse on Boone** are hybrid in a different way: sourdough + chemical leaveners (baking powder/soda). This is actually closer to the ATK original, which uses baking powder + yeast.

**Buttermilk by Sam** (data incomplete but confirmed from search snippets): Uses tangzhong + sourdough starter + active dry yeast. This is a TRUE hybrid and the most relevant reference for our adaptation. Key details from what was confirmed:
- Uses bread flour
- Uses tangzhong (milk bread technique)
- Uses both active sourdough starter AND active dry yeast
- First rise: 4-6 hours
- Bake temp: likely 350F (based on her other recipes)
- Heavy cream bath for gooey texture

## Key Takeaways for Our ATK Adaptation

1. **Starter amount**: 100-227g range. For a 390g flour recipe like ATK, ~100-150g starter is the sweet spot (~25-38% of flour weight).

2. **Keep or drop the yeast?**: King Arthur and Buttermilk by Sam both show that sourdough + commercial yeast works. For reliability and a shorter timeline, keeping a small amount of yeast (1 tsp) is defensible. Pure sourdough is more "authentic" but requires 8-12+ hours.

3. **Flour swap**: Consider bread flour instead of AP for better structure with enrichments.

4. **Butter level**: ATK's 140g butter is right in line with The Perfect Loaf (139g). No need to reduce.

5. **Egg addition**: ATK original has no eggs. Adding 1 egg (King Arthur model) adds richness and tenderness. Adding 3 eggs (Perfect Loaf model) makes it full brioche.

6. **Baking powder decision**: ATK uses 2.5 tsp baking powder. Options:
   - Drop it entirely (pure sourdough rise)
   - Reduce to 1 tsp (insurance)
   - Keep it (Little Spoon Farm / Farmhouse on Boone model -- add after overnight ferment)

7. **Timeline**: Plan for overnight or 6+ hour same-day. The 30-minute ATK rise won't work with sourdough.

8. **Bake temp**: Consider bumping from ATK's 350F to 375-400F given the enriched sourdough dough.

9. **Tangzhong option**: Buttermilk by Sam and Halicopter Away both use tangzhong for extra softness. This is an optional enhancement that could replace cornstarch (which ATK doesn't use but our adapted recipe might).
