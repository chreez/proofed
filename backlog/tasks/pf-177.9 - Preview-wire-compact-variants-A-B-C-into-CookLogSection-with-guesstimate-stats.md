## v3 Iteration (confidence rating + 2026-04-06 temp fix)

### Schema change

Added an optional `confidence?: 'high' | 'medium' | 'low'` field to `BakeStatsBlock` in `src/types/recipe.ts`. Non-breaking additive change. Semantics documented in JSDoc:

- `high` — logged in real-time, reliable
- `medium` — mix of logged and recalled, directionally accurate
- `low` — recalled or guesstimate, user explicitly flagged as unreliable, excluded from comparisons
- `undefined` — unknown (UI treats as implicitly medium; older entries predate this field and should not be rewritten)

## v4 Iteration (low-confidence falls back to recipe defaults)

Third round of HITL feedback. The v3 treatment was wrong direction: greying out stored guesstimate values while still displaying them is confusing, and for bakes far in the past the stored values may bear no relation to what the recipe actually prescribed. v4 replaces the grey-and-caveat approach with a hard fallback to recipe defaults.

### User feedback (verbatim)

> "in low confidence - it should default back to what the recipe says at that version. since we are pretty far ahead we may not have easy access to the version bakes. its still low confidence if it wasn't directly logged by the user"

### Interpretation

- `confidence: 'low'` semantics updated: "data wasn't directly logged by the user" (guesstimate or recall)
- Low-confidence entries should DISPLAY recipe defaults at that version instead of the stored guesstimates
- Historical version snapshots aren't available — use CURRENT recipe as the proxy
- The low-conf flag still applies (entry is still flagged as untrustworthy and visually distinct)
- Stored `bake_stats` data is PRESERVED in JSON for future PF-177.7 audit but NOT used for display when `confidence === 'low'`

### Schema addition

New `RecipeBakeDefaults` interface added to `src/types/recipe.ts`, attached as an optional `bake_defaults?` field on `Recipe`:

```ts
/**
 * Recipe-level default bake params. Used as the fallback when a cook log entry
 * has low-confidence bake_stats (data wasn't directly logged by the user).
 * These represent "what the recipe prescribes" at the current recipe version.
 * Historical version snapshots aren't tracked — treat as current baseline.
 */
export interface RecipeBakeDefaults {
  bake_phases?: BakePhase[]
}
```

Non-breaking additive change. Only `bake_phases` populated in v4 — bulk/proof fallbacks intentionally NOT provided because the recipe doesn't prescribe specific bulk/proof durations (it's a variable based on dough temp and rise tracking).

### Recipe-level defaults on simple-sourdough.json

Added top-level `bake_defaults` block extracted from the recipe's current BAKE states. Values derived from state `direction` prose:

| Phase | temp_f | duration_min | Source prose |
|---|---|---|---|
| preheat | 500 | 45 | PREHEAT state: *"Preheat to 500°F (260°C) for at least 45 minutes."* |
| covered | 450 | 20 | BAKE_COVERED state: *"Reduce temperature to 450°F (230°C). Bake covered for 20 minutes."* |
| uncovered | 425 | 20 | BAKE_UNCOVERED state: *"Reduce temperature to 425-450°F (218-232°C). Bake uncovered 9 minutes, flip the loaf for even browning, bake another 9 minutes."* |

**Decisions on ambiguity:**

- **Uncovered temp**: recipe prose uses a range `425-450°F`. Picked **425°F** as the canonical value — the prose says "reduce temperature to…" which implies bringing it DOWN from the 450°F covered setpoint; the low end of the range is the clearer default.
- **Uncovered duration**: recipe prose is 9 min + flip + 9 min (18 min of bake time plus flip handling). The state's `duration_min` field is 20. Used **20** to match the state's declared duration.

No recipe version bump, no `change_log` entry (field is additive and data is still preview).

### Decisions made (v4)

- **Bake no longer protected.** In v3, the bake pill stayed full-opacity because "bake duration is still reliable per user carve-out." In v4, the bake pill ALSO gets the grey class because the displayed values come from recipe baseline, not the user. Consistency over carve-out — if it's not user-logged, it's greyed.
- **Uncovered canonical temp = 425°F**, not 450°F. The prose says "reduce temperature to 425-450°F" from the 450°F covered setpoint — "reduce" implies going below, so the lower bound is the more accurate interpretation of "what the recipe prescribes."
- **Uncovered canonical duration = 20m**, matching the state's `duration_min` field (not the 9+flip+9 = 18m from the prose). The state-level declaration is the machine-readable source of truth.
- **Bulk / proof don't get recipe defaults.** Recipe bulk/proof times are highly variable (dough temp, ambient, starter activity), not prescribed by the recipe. Fallback is `—` with a `not directly logged` tooltip rather than manufacturing a fake value.
- **Defensive fallback when recipe has no `bake_defaults`**: bake also renders `—`. This keeps the contract clean — if the fallback source is missing, nothing is shown rather than accidentally leaking the stored guesstimate data.
- **Recipe defaults are version-agnostic.** v4 treats them as "current baseline" per the user's note: "we are pretty far ahead we may not have easy access to the version bakes." No version-pinned history of defaults — when the recipe changes, ALL low-conf historical bakes update their displayed bake temps to the new current defaults. This is a known trade-off and documented in the JSDoc.
- **Helper simplification.** Dropped `withLowConfidenceCaveat` from v3 — v4's two tooltip strings are fixed phrases (`"not directly logged"`, `"X (recipe baseline)"`), cleaner to inline.
