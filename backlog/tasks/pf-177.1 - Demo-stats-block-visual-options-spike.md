### Three variants rendered side-by-side
- **Variant A — Horizontal bar + disclosure**: 1-line mono summary bar (yield / bulk / dough °F / final °F / cost / rating), with S&F, bake params, and aliquot in collapsible `<details>` blocks beneath. Density champion.
- **Variant B — 2-column compact grid**: Splits inputs (S&F table, bake params dl) from results (yield/bulk/cost/rating dl, aliquot table) into side-by-side columns. Always-visible sub-blocks, higher per-card footprint. Stacks to single column on mobile.
- **Variant C — Vertical list with tiles + stacked blocks**: 4 headline tiles (yield, bulk, dough avg, cost), then three stacked structured sub-blocks (S&F grid, bake params 2-col, aliquot rise with visual bar). Mobile-first: 2×2 tiles on narrow viewports, single-column blocks everywhere.
