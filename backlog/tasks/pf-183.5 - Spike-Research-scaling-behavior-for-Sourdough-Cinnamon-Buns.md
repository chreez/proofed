## Scaling Behavior Analysis — Sourdough Cinnamon Buns

### Ingredient Classification

**Levain Stage:**
| Ingredient | ID | Amount | Classification | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ripe Sourdough Starter | starter | 34g | non_linear | Levain seed — at 2x, 34g still seeds 370g+ levain adequately. No need to double. At 0.5x, reduce to ~20g. |
| AP Flour (levain) | levain_flour | 84g | linear | Must scale with levain water to maintain 100% hydration |
| Water (levain) | levain_water | 84g | linear | Must scale with levain flour |

**Main Dough:**
| Ingredient | ID | Amount | Classification | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| All-Purpose Flour | flour | 430g | linear | Base structural component |
| Cornstarch | cornstarch | 64g | linear | Gluten dilution ratio must remain constant |
| Granulated Sugar | granulated_sugar | 75g | linear | Dough sweetness scales with flour |
| Light Brown Sugar | brown_sugar | 240g | linear | Filling coverage scales with dough area |
| Confectioners' Sugar | powdered_sugar | 128g | linear | Glaze scales with bun count |
| Unsalted Butter | butter | 227g | linear | Enrichment ratio must stay constant |
| Whole Milk | milk | 95g | linear | Hydration component |
| Cream Cheese | cream_cheese | 150g | linear | Glaze scales with bun count |
| Large Eggs | eggs | 3 whole | linear | Enrichment scales with flour (note: at 0.5x = 1.5 eggs — use 2 and slightly reduce milk) |
| Ground Cinnamon | cinnamon | 10g | linear | Filling flavor |
| Salt | salt | 11g | linear | Baker's percentage maintained |
| Vanilla Extract | vanilla | 5g | linear | Glaze flavor |
| Heavy Cream | heavy_cream | 100g | linear | Caramel bottom scales with pan area |

### Tested Multiplier Range
- **Min**: 0.5x (4 buns — fits a 15-18cm round pan)
- **Max**: 2x (16 buns — requires two 23cm springform pans or one large pan)

### Process Caveats
1. **Pan sizing**: 1x = 23cm springform (8 buns). At 2x, use two springforms or a 33x23cm sheet pan. Buns need ~1cm spacing before proof.
2. **Levain timing**: Levain amount is non-linear with batch size — the 200g levain used in the dough scales linearly, but the starter seed for building the levain does not need to double.
3. **Cold retard**: Larger dough mass takes longer to chill through. At 2x, allow an extra hour of cold retard for the center to reach fridge temp, or divide dough into two bowls.
4. **Stand mixer capacity**: At 2x, most 5-6 qt mixers are at capacity with enriched dough. Mix in two batches if necessary.
5. **Rolling**: At 2x, the dough sheet is 92x46cm — very large. Consider dividing dough in half and making two separate rolls.
6. **Bake time**: Two pans in the oven may need rotation and 3-5 min extra time. Use internal temp (190°F / 88°C) as primary indicator.
