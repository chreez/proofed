## Scaling Behavior Analysis — ATK Ultimate Cinnamon Buns

### Ingredient Classification

| Ingredient | ID | Amount | Classification | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Unsalted Butter | butter | 227g | linear | Fat coverage and dough enrichment scale with dough mass |
| All-Purpose Flour | flour | 531g | linear | Base structural component |
| Cornstarch | cornstarch | 64g | linear | Gluten dilution ratio must remain constant for tenderness |
| Granulated Sugar | granulated_sugar | 75g | linear | Dough sweetness and yeast food scale with flour |
| Light Brown Sugar | brown_sugar | 240g | linear | Filling coverage scales with dough surface area (which scales linearly with batch) |
| Confectioners' Sugar | powdered_sugar | 128g | linear | Glaze volume scales with number of buns |
| Whole Milk | milk | 192g | linear | Hydration component scales with flour |
| Cream Cheese | cream_cheese | 150g | linear | Glaze coverage scales with number of buns |
| Large Eggs | eggs | 3 whole | linear | Enrichment scales with flour weight |
| Instant Yeast | yeast | 7g | non_linear | At 2x, yeast could be reduced ~10-15% since larger dough mass retains more heat, accelerating fermentation. At 0.5x, keep proportional or add slightly more. |
| Ground Cinnamon | cinnamon | 10g | linear | Filling flavor scales with filling mass |
| Salt | salt | 11g | linear | Baker's % of flour must remain constant |
| Vanilla Extract | vanilla | 5g | linear | Glaze flavor scales with glaze mass |

### Tested Multiplier Range
- **Min**: 0.5x (4 buns — half batch, fits a 20cm round pan)
- **Max**: 2x (16 buns — double batch, requires two 33x23cm pans or one large sheet pan)

### Process Caveats
1. **Pan size**: 1x = 33x23cm pan (8 buns). At 2x, use two pans or a full sheet pan. Buns must not be overcrowded or they'll merge into a single mass with raw centers.
2. **Rise time adjusts**: Larger dough mass ferments slightly faster due to thermal mass. At 2x, check first rise 15-20 min earlier. At 0.5x, may need 15-30 min longer.
3. **Stand mixer capacity**: Most home stand mixers (5-6 qt) can handle up to 1.5x. At 2x, mix in two batches or use a larger mixer.
4. **Bake time**: At 2x in a single large pan, bake time increases 5-10 min. Use internal temp (190°F / 88°C) as the true indicator, not time.
5. **Rolling surface**: At 2x, the dough sheet is much larger — ensure adequate counter space and rolling surface.
6. **Glaze**: Scales perfectly linearly — no process changes needed.
