## Scaling Behavior Analysis — Sourdough Chocolate Chip Cookies

### Ingredient Classification

| Ingredient | ID | Amount | Classification | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Unsalted Butter | butter | 225g | linear | Brown butter yield scales linearly |
| Dark Brown Sugar | dark-brown-sugar | 210g | linear | Sugar ratio must stay constant for texture |
| Granulated Sugar | granulated-sugar | 96g | linear | Crispness/spread balance |
| Egg Yolks | egg-yolks | 54g (3 yolks) | linear | At 0.5x = 1.5 yolks — use 2 yolks (36g). At 2x = 6 yolks. |
| Sourdough Discard | sourdough-discard | 150g | linear | Moisture and flavor contribution scales with flour |
| Vanilla Extract | vanilla | 13g | linear | Flavor scales with batch |
| All-Purpose Flour | ap-flour | 200g | linear | Base structural component |
| Bread Flour | bread-flour | 120g | linear | Chewiness contribution scales with total flour |
| Baking Soda | baking-soda | 5g | non_linear | Chemical leavener — at 2x, use 9g (not 10g). Over-leavening causes cookies to puff then collapse flat. At 0.5x, use 3g. |
| Baking Powder | baking-powder | 2g | non_linear | Insurance lift — at 2x, use 3.5g (not 4g). Same caution as baking soda. |
| Fine Sea Salt | fine-sea-salt | 6g | linear | Flavor balance |
| Chopped Chocolate | chocolate | 350g | linear | Chocolate-to-dough ratio is a defining characteristic |
| Flaky Sea Salt | flaky-salt | 2g | fixed | Garnish — amount per cookie is fixed regardless of batch size. Scale by cookie count, not dough volume. |

### Tested Multiplier Range
- **Min**: 0.5x (~11-12 cookies — good for testing/small batch)
- **Max**: 3x (~66-72 cookies — large batch for events, freeze extras)

### Process Caveats
1. **Brown butter batch**: At 2x+, brown butter in a larger saucepan. Deeper butter pool = more uneven browning. Stir more frequently. At 3x, consider browning in two batches.
2. **Chilling time scales**: Larger volume of dough takes longer to chill to center. At 2x, add 2-4 hours to overnight rest. At 3x, divide dough into two containers.
3. **Baking is sequential**: 6 cookies per sheet, one sheet at a time. At 3x (72 cookies), plan for ~3 hours of active baking. Let sheets cool between batches.
4. **Freezing**: Extra dough balls freeze perfectly. Scoop, freeze on sheet, transfer to zip bag. Bake from frozen +2-3 min. This is the primary scaling strategy — make a big batch, freeze most.
5. **Mixing**: Hand-mix works at 2x. At 3x, the dough volume may be unwieldy — consider a stand mixer on low.
6. **Chemical leaveners**: Baking soda and baking powder do NOT scale perfectly linearly. At 2x+, reduce by ~10% to avoid over-leavening (puffy then flat cookies).
