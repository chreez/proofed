## Scaling Behavior Analysis — Sourdough Bread 70% Hydration

### Ingredient Classification

| Ingredient | ID | Amount | Classification | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bread Flour | bread_flour | 1000g | linear | Base structural component, baker's math |
| Water | water | 700g | linear | 70% hydration ratio must be maintained |
| Active Sourdough Starter | starter | 200g | non_linear | 20% inoculation. At 2x, could reduce to 15-18% (300-360g instead of 400g) since larger dough mass retains heat and ferments faster. At 0.5x, keep at 20% or increase slightly to 22%. |
| Fine Sea Salt | salt | 20g | linear | 2% baker's percentage must be maintained |

### Tested Multiplier Range
- **Min**: 0.5x (1 loaf — single Dutch oven bake, no divide step needed)
- **Max**: 2x (4 loaves — requires 2 bannetons and sequential baking in pairs)

### Process Caveats
1. **Dutch oven capacity**: 1 loaf per Dutch oven per bake. At 2x (4 loaves), you need 4 sequential bakes or 2 Dutch ovens. Each bake cycle is ~45 min. Second pair continues proofing — risk of over-proofing.
2. **Bulk fermentation timing**: Larger dough mass (2x = 3.8kg) generates more metabolic heat. Expect bulk to complete 1-2 hours earlier than the standard 10-12 hour overnight. Use aliquot jar, not clock.
3. **Banneton capacity**: Standard round banneton holds ~800-900g dough. At 2x, divide into 4 pieces of ~475g each (smaller loaves) or 2 pieces of ~950g (standard size). Recipe's current divide target is ~950g per loaf.
4. **Mixing bowl size**: At 2x, need a very large bowl (8L+) for stretch and folds. Dough at 2x is ~3.8kg before baking.
5. **Scoring and loading**: Cold dough from fridge is easier to score. With 4 loaves, keep batches 3-4 in fridge while baking 1-2.
6. **At 0.5x**: Skip the DIVIDE step entirely. Shape one loaf directly. Single Dutch oven bake — simplest workflow.

### Sources
1. The Perfect Loaf — sourdough scaling and bulk fermentation guidelines
2. The Sourdough Journey — fermentation timing by dough temperature
3. User cook_log data from 14 bakes at standard 2-loaf yield confirming process parameters
