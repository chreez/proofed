## Scaling Behavior Analysis — NY-Style Pizza Dough

### Ingredient Classification

| Ingredient | ID | Amount | Classification | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bread Flour | bread_flour | 463g | linear | Base structural component |
| Water | water | 288g | linear | 62% hydration ratio must be maintained |
| Instant Dry Yeast | instant_yeast | 6g | non_linear | At 2x, reduce to ~10g (not 12g) — larger dough mass ferments faster from thermal mass. At 4x, use ~16g not 24g. |
| Olive Oil | olive_oil | 18g | linear | Enrichment ratio scales with flour |
| Salt | salt | 8g | linear | ~1.7% baker's percentage maintained |
| Cornmeal | cornmeal | 13g | fixed | Peel dusting — amount depends on number of pizza launches, not dough volume. ~7g per pizza. |
| San Marzano Sauce | san_marzano_sauce | 175g | linear | ~88g per pizza, scales with pizza count |
| Low-Moisture Mozzarella | mozzarella | 350g | linear | ~175g per pizza, scales with pizza count |
| Hard Cheese | hard_cheese | 40g | linear | ~20g per pizza, scales with pizza count |
| Pepperoni | pepperoni | 175g | linear | ~88g per pizza, scales with pizza count |

### Tested Multiplier Range
- **Min**: 1x (2 pizzas — base recipe)
- **Max**: 4x (8 pizzas / 8 dough balls — freezes well, common batch size)

### Process Caveats
1. **Stand mixer capacity**: Most 5-6 qt mixers handle up to 2x (4 pizzas). At 3-4x, mix in batches or use a larger mixer. Over-filling causes uneven kneading.
2. **Cold proof space**: Each dough ball needs its own container or tray space. At 4x, you need 2-3 sheet trays or 8 individual containers in the fridge.
3. **Baking is sequential**: Only 1 pizza bakes at a time on a single steel. At 4x, plan for ~1 hour of active baking. Steel temperature drops after each pizza — allow 10-15 min recovery between pies.
4. **Dough ball weight**: Maintain ~383g per ball regardless of multiplier. Divide total dough by number of desired pizzas.
5. **Freezing surplus**: Dough balls freeze excellently after the cold bulk ferment + divide step. Thaw in fridge overnight, then temper 1 hour before use.
6. **Topping scaling**: Scale toppings per pizza (88g sauce, 175g mozz, 20g parm, 88g pepperoni) — these are per-unit, not bulk amounts.
