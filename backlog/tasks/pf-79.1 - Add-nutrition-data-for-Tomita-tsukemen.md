## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Calculate nutrition data for Tomita tsukemen recipe via USDA pipeline (same pattern as PF-70.1, PF-71.2, PF-78.1).

Notable ingredients needing mappings:
- Pork femur/neck bones, chicken feet, chicken backs (collagen extraction — model what ends up in broth vs discarded)
- Niboshi (dried sardines), katsuobushi (dried bonito), sababushi (dried mackerel)
- Gyofun (fish powder blend — 40% mackerel, 30% bonito, 20% sardine, 10% flounder)
- Kansui (negligible calories)
- Ki-joyu / koikuchi soy sauce, hon-mirin

Challenges:
- 6+ sub-recipes (animal broth, fish dashi, tare, aroma oil, noodles, gyofun) that combine into per-bowl portions
- Soup-wari dilution adds additional broth — should be counted separately or as optional
- 200g noodle portion (larger than typical ramen) significantly impacts per-serving carbs
- Fish dashi extraction: dried fish is steeped and strained — need to estimate what transfers to liquid
