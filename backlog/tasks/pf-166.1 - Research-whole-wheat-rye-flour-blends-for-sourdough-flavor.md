## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Use /research skill to determine the best flour blend for a flavor-focused sourdough variant. Base recipe is simple-sourdough (70% hydration, 20% inoculation, 1000g flour for 2 loaves).

Research questions:
- What % of whole wheat gives the biggest flavor improvement without wrecking handling? (Common ranges: 10-30%)
- Does adding a small % of rye (5-10%) meaningfully improve flavor beyond whole wheat alone?
- How much does hydration need to increase per % of whole grain? (Bran absorbs more water)
- Does fermentation timing change? (Whole grain ferments faster due to wild yeast on bran)
- Any shaping or scoring differences needed?
- What do experienced bakers recommend as a "best first step" blend for someone coming from 100% bread flour?

Output: documented findings in parent task notes, with specific ratio recommendation and adjusted numbers ready for recipe creation.
