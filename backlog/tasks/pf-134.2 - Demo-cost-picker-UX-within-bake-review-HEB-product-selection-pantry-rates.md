## Design Decisions (2026-02-15)

**Product picker: Cards (Variant B)** — larger selectable cards with product details, check mark on selection.

**Pantry rate mode: Approved** — toggle between fresh purchase and stored rate.

**Cost summary: Three tiers**
1. **Supplies total** — what you actually paid at checkout (full package prices)
2. **Bake cost** — proportional amount used from packages
3. **Per serving** — bake cost ÷ servings

Example: bought butter 4ct for $3.98 (supplies), used 140g of 454g = $1.23 (bake cost), ÷8 servings = $0.15/serving. Supplies total captures that you might get 2+ batches from the same purchases.
