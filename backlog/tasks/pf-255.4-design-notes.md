> Design doc for the drag-to-queue bake scheduler. Companion demo:
> `public/demo/pricing-scheduler.html` (open via dev server at
> `http://<lan>:6811/demo/pricing-scheduler.html`).

## 1. Problem Framing

A home baker with one oven needs to plan a multi-recipe bake day (often a
weekend market run) and answer two questions:

1. **Can I physically fit these bakes in my window?** (oven contention,
   active-prep collisions, sleep schedule)
2. **Am I making money?** Profit-per-hour both *active* (labor) and
   *elapsed* (calendar time) drives whether to bake overnight or take a
   2am wake-up.

Each recipe decomposes into three phase types:

| Phase | Attention | Oven? | Examples |
|-------|-----------|-------|----------|
| `prep_active` | Hands-on | No | Mixing, shaping, glazing |
| `prep_passive` | Hands-off | No | Bulk ferment, cold retard, autolyse rest |
| `bake` | Hands-off | **Yes** | Oven occupancy |

The scheduler reasons about these three independently — oven is the only
exclusive resource; passive proofs can overlap freely.

## 2. Window Choice: Multi-Day (36h horizon)

**Decision: 36-hour rolling window, not single-day.**

### Why not single-day

Every flagship recipe in this notebook has a meaningful overnight phase:

- **ATK Cinnamon Buns (Overnight)** — 8h cold retard between Day 1 and Day 2
- **Sourdough Choc Chip Cookies** — 16h overnight rest
- **Tartine Baguette** — 12h cold bulk
- **Sourdough discard cookies** — fits same-day but rarely planned alone

A single-day timeline either lies about these recipes (truncates the
overnight phase) or refuses to schedule them. Both are worse than just
showing 36 hours.

### Why not full-week

Profit-per-hour intuition collapses past ~2 days — bakers don't think
"market is Saturday so I'll start Wednesday." They think "I bake Friday
night, finish Saturday morning." A 36h window covers the entire
overnight-into-morning pattern without becoming a calendar app.

### 36h rationale

- Anchored at user-picked start (e.g., "Friday 4pm")
- Ends 36h later (Sunday 4am-ish) — comfortably covers a Friday-night
  start to a Saturday-morning market bake
- Visualized as two horizontal day-bands stacked (or one 36h ruler with a
  midnight tick)

### Trade-offs

- **Pro:** All current recipes fit. Overnight rest is visible, not hidden.
- **Pro:** "Started prep too early on Friday" mistakes show up.
- **Con:** Pixel budget per hour is tighter. Mitigated by compressed
  rendering for passive segments (§5).

## 3. Drag-to-Queue Interaction Spec

### Layout

```
┌─────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ RECIPE LIBRARY (left)   │ TIMELINE (right, 36h horizontal)           │
│                         │                                            │
│ [ ] Cinnamon Buns ON    │ Fri 4p  8p  12a  4a   8a  12p  4p  Sat..   │
│ [ ] SD Choc Cookies     │ ├────────┼─────┼────────┼─────────┼────┤  │
│ [ ] Focaccia            │ │OVEN: ▓▓▓                                │
│ [ ] Baguette            │ │ACTIVE:                                   │
│                         │ │PASSIVE: ░░░░░░░                          │
│ [Drag any to →]         │                                            │
└─────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Drag mechanics

1. **Pick up:** mousedown on a recipe card in the library. Ghost block
   shows the recipe's full duration as a translucent overlay following
   the cursor. Block is sized to the recipe's `total_elapsed_min`.
2. **Drag over timeline:** the ghost snaps to **15-minute increments**
   on the time axis. Hour-level snap is too coarse (proofs end on
   half-hours often); 5-minute is too jittery. 15 min hits the sweet
   spot.
3. **Live conflict highlight:** while dragging, any oven-phase
   collision in the proposed placement turns **red** (danger
   `#a65252`). Active-prep collisions turn **amber** (warning
   `#c9a66b`) — these aren't impossible, just unpleasant.
4. **Drop:**
   - Drop on a **valid slot** → block lands, snaps to 15-min grid.
   - Drop on **oven conflict** → block stays attached to cursor with
     a shake animation; refuses to land. User must move it.
   - Drop on **active-prep conflict** → block lands but stays amber;
     user can dismiss with a "I'll multitask" toggle on the block.
5. **Reposition:** dropped blocks can be grabbed by their **start-edge
   handle** and slid. Same snap rules apply.
6. **Remove:** click block → side panel → "Remove" button. (No drag-off
   gesture; too easy to misfire.)

### Snap-to-availability (auto-suggest)

When the user drops a block in a clearly broken position (e.g., oven
overlap), the scheduler offers a **"Snap to next free slot →"** chip
that, if clicked, slides the bake forward in time until oven is free.

### Out of scope for v1

- Drag-to-resize (changing a recipe's duration). Recipes are immutable
  blocks; durations come from the JSON.
- Multi-select drag.
- Dragging between days (works naturally since timeline is continuous).

## 4. Profit/Hour Overlay Calc

Two metrics, shown side-by-side because they tell different stories.

### 4.1 Active profit/hour (labor efficiency)

```
$/hr active = Σ(yield × unit_price) / Σ(prep_active_min) × 60
```

- **Numerator:** Sum of `yield × pricePerUnit` over all scheduled bakes.
  Pulls from `cook_log.entry.cost.pricePerUnit` if present, else from
  PF-255.1 pricing snapshot.
- **Denominator:** Sum of all `prep_active` minutes across scheduled
  bakes. Passive and bake phases are excluded — the baker isn't working
  during those.

This is the headline number for "is this worth my time?"

### 4.2 Elapsed profit/hour (calendar efficiency)

```
$/hr elapsed = Σ(revenue) / (window_end - window_start) × 60
```

- **Numerator:** Same as above.
- **Denominator:** Wall-clock duration from first-bake-start to
  last-bake-out-of-oven.

This catches the "I spent 24 hours on this but only 3 active" story.
When overnight bakes are queued, elapsed $/hr tanks; this is **fine** —
it's what overnight bakes do. The number is informational, not a goal
to maximize. A baker comparing "should I do overnight cold retard or
wake up at 4am same-day?" reads:

- Overnight: low elapsed $/hr, high active $/hr (you slept)
- Same-day: high elapsed $/hr, similar active $/hr (no sleep penalty
  *unless* it's a 2am alarm — see §6)

### 4.3 Per-recipe profit/hour breakdown

A small table under the main number:

```
RECIPE                  REVENUE   ACTIVE  ELAPSED  $/hr A  $/hr E
Cinnamon Buns (ON)      $48       2.5h    14h      $19     $3.4
SD Choc Cookies         $32       1.3h    18h      $25     $1.8
Focaccia                $24       1h      6h       $24     $4.0
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TOTAL                   $104      4.8h    24h      $22     $4.3
```

The per-recipe view answers "which bake is dragging the average down?"

### 4.4 Edge cases

- **Bakes outside the window** → excluded from both metrics.
- **No price set** → recipe contributes hours but $0 revenue. UI shows
  a `pricing missing` warning chip on the block.
- **Overlapping passive phases** → no special handling. Elapsed time is
  wall-clock; overlap shortens elapsed (good) without changing active
  (correct).

## 5. Overnight-Proof Visualization

The problem: an 8-hour cold retard on a 36h timeline at ~25px/hour =
200px of empty box. Boring at best, illegible if the segment is
zoomed-out further. Three techniques, used together:

### 5.1 Compressed passive segments (the key idea)

Passive segments **collapse to a fixed 60px width** with a "zigzag" or
"break" marker showing the segment is time-compressed. The actual
duration is rendered as a label inside:

```
PREP ▓▓▓▓│≈≈≈≈ COLD RETARD 8h ≈≈≈≈│▓▓ SHAPE │░░░ PROOF 1h ░░░│▓▓▓ BAKE
```

- **Active phases** render at true scale (so the baker can see how a
  20-min shape compares to a 5-min mix).
- **Passive phases** render at true scale **up to 90 minutes**; longer
  passive phases collapse to a 60px "≈ Xh ≈" pill.
- Toggling **"True scale"** mode in the toolbar disables compression
  for users who want literal time.

### 5.2 Dotted vs solid borders

- **Solid 2px border:** active prep or oven (you must be present)
- **Dotted 2px border:** passive (you can sleep / leave / do other
  things)
- **Cross-hatched fill:** cold retard specifically (refrigerator
  resource — flagged so two cold retards on the same day get a "fridge
  capacity?" gentle nudge)

### 5.3 Color coding by phase

| Phase type | Color | CSS var | Notes |
|------------|-------|---------|-------|
| prep_active | `#a67c52` (crust) | `--color-crust` | Warm tan, draws eye |
| prep_passive (rt) | `#d4cdc1` (stone-300) | `--color-stone-300` | Recedes |
| prep_passive (cold) | `#d0d8e8` (custom blue-grey) | new token | Implies fridge |
| bake | `#a65d45` (accent) | `--color-accent` | The expensive resource |
| conflict | `#a65252` (danger) | `--color-danger` | Oven double-book |
| warning | `#c9a66b` (warning) | `--color-warning` | Active-prep overlap |

### 5.4 Day band

A subtle horizontal stripe behind the timeline darkens during
nighttime hours (10pm-6am) to visually anchor "this is when normal
humans sleep." See §6 for the alarm hooks.

## 6. 2am Wake-up Surfacing

When a bake places an **active-prep phase between 10pm and 6am**, the
scheduler treats this as a soft alarm event.

### Detection

For each scheduled block:

1. Iterate over its phases.
2. For any `prep_active` or `bake-end` (oven-pull) phase whose start
   falls in `[22:00, 06:00)` local time:
   - Tag the block with `alarm: { time, reason }`.
   - Show a small **alarm icon (◴ or ⏰)** on the block.
   - In the side detail panel, list "Wake-up required at 4:12am — pull
     bake from oven."

### Surfacing levels

| Hour | Severity | UI treatment |
|------|----------|--------------|
| 6am–8am | "early" | Yellow dot, no warning |
| 4am–6am | "rough" | Amber border on alarm icon, tooltip says "Early start" |
| 12am–4am | "brutal" | Red border on alarm icon, **toolbar banner** asks "Move overnight start later?" with a one-click +2h shift |
| 10pm–12am | "late" | Same as 4-6am — amber, but framed as "stay up late" |

### Auto-suggest fix

When the toolbar banner fires for a brutal alarm, clicking "Suggest
overnight option" searches the recipe for a sibling variant with a
cold-retard step (matching by name prefix or `meta.variant_of`). If
found, offers to swap. Example:

> "ATK Cinnamon Buns (Quick) needs a 3:40am start to be ready by 8am.
> Switch to ATK Cinnamon Buns (Overnight)? Start Friday 6pm instead."

### Out of scope for v1

- Actual alarm clock integration (calendar export, push notifs).
- Sleep-debt scoring across consecutive days.

## 7. Demo States (rendered in `pricing-scheduler.html`)

The demo is static — no real drag — but shows four key visual states:

1. **Empty timeline** — placeholder text, recipe library visible left.
2. **Populated 3-bake queue** — Cinnamon Buns ON + SD Choc Cookies +
   Focaccia, with profit/hour readouts top-right.
3. **Overnight cold retard** — compressed passive segment with
   "≈ 8h ≈" pill, cross-hatched, dotted border.
4. **Conflict warning** — a fourth recipe ghost-dropped showing red
   oven overlap and the "Snap to next free slot →" chip.

Plus an inline legend explaining phase colors and an alarm icon
demonstration on the SD Choc Cookies bake (which requires a 5am pull).

## 8. Open Questions (for follow-up tasks)

- **Pricing source of truth:** Does the scheduler read from PF-255.1
  pricing snapshot, or per-bake `cook_log.cost.pricePerUnit`? Pricing
  spike PF-255.2 likely answers.
- **Recipe metadata audit (PF-255.6):** Do all recipes have
  `phases[]` declared in JSON, or does the scheduler infer phases from
  state `timer` + `duration_min`? Inference is fine for v1 but
  authored phases would let the baker mark "this passive step is
  half-attended" (vs sleep-through).
- **Persistence:** Where does the scheduled queue live? Local
  storage? A profile JSON (PF-255.5)? Markup says profile.
- **Mobile:** Drag is awkward on touch. Mobile gets a tap-to-add modal
  with time-picker — not in demo.
