## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Bake one sourdough loaf pair from the current v3.2.0 baseline with exactly one variable changed: the uncovered-phase oven temperature raised to a sustained 475°F (232°C). All other parameters held constant — 500°F preheat, 450°F covered bake ~22 min, uncovered duration 18 min (9 min + flip + 9 min), pull at 210°F internal, standard wire-rack cool.

## Hypothesis

Raising uncovered temperature from 425-450°F to 475°F will produce a thinner, crispier, more crackly crust with no crumb regression. Source rationale: The Perfect Loaf + The Sourdough Journey agree that higher temp + shorter uncovered time produces thinner crispier crusts in this range.

## Variables

**Changed:** Uncovered bake temperature → 475°F (vs. 425-450°F baseline)

**Held constant:**
- 500°F preheat (25 min minimum)
- 450°F covered bake for 22 min
- Uncovered duration: 18 min (9 min + flip + 9 min pattern from v3.2.0)
- Pull at 210°F internal temperature
- Standard wire-rack cool (no finish-cold yet — that's Experiment 2)
- 70% hydration, 100% bread flour, 20% inoculation, fermentolyse, cold overnight proof
- Sequential 2-loaf bake

## Measurement rubric (1-5 scale unless noted)

- **Crust thickness** (thin / medium / thick — subjective mouthfeel when biting)
- **Crust crispness** (audible crack when squeezed or sliced? 1-5)
- **Crust crispness retention at 2hr** (1-5)
- **Crust crispness retention at 6hr** (1-5)
- **Crumb quality** (no regressions: open, tender, not gummy — pass/fail)
- **Overall preference** (ranked vs. Bake 12 and prior bakes)

Log all 6 metrics in the cook_log entry with a reference to PF-178.2.

## Pass criteria

- Crust crispness ≥4/5 (vs. Bake 12's informal ~3/5)
- Crispness retention at 2hr ≥3/5
- Crumb quality pass (no gummy/underbaked regression)

## Fail criteria

- Crust burns or goes bitter before internal temp hits 210°F
- Crumb dries out from the higher heat
- Oven spring impeded by crust setting too fast

## Parent context

PF-178.1 spike identified this as the HIGHEST confidence × HIGHEST impact experiment. Our v3.2.0 baseline sits at the LOW end of the recommended uncovered range (425-450°F vs. consensus 450-475°F for crispier thinner crust).
