## Research questions (from parent)

1. What's the actual driver of crust crispness — total energy into the crust, final surface moisture, sugar caramelization, starch gelatinization, or a combination?
2. Higher temp + shorter vs. lower temp + longer — which produces a crispier crust for this dough?
3. Does finishing cold (leaving the oven cracked during cooldown) help?
4. Does a light second-stage spritz or splash of water extend the steam phase differently than just the Dutch oven lid?
5. What's the role of internal dough temperature in final crust texture?
6. How does post-bake handling affect final crust crispness?
