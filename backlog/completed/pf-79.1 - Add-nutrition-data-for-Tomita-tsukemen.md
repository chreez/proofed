## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Calculate nutrition data for Tomita tsukemen recipe via USDA pipeline (same pattern as PF-70.1, PF-71.2, PF-78.1).

Notable ingredients needing mappings:
- Pork femur/neck bones, chicken feet, chicken backs (collagen extraction — model what ends up in broth vs discarded)
- Niboshi (dried sardines), katsuobushi (dried bonito), sababushi (dried mackerel)
- Gyofun (fish powder blend — 40% mackerel, 30% bonito, 20% sardine, 10% flounder)
- Kansui (negligible calories)
- Ki-joyu / koikuchi soy sauce, hon-mirin

Challenges:
- 6+ sub-recipes (animal broth, fish dashi, tare, aroma oil, noodles, gyofun) that combine into per-bowl portions
- Soup-wari dilution adds additional broth — should be counted separately or as optional
- 200g noodle portion (larger than typical ramen) significantly impacts per-serving carbs
- Fish dashi extraction: dried fish is steeped and strained — need to estimate what transfers to liquid

## Final Summary

Added nutrition data for Tomita/Fuunji-Style Gyokai-Tonkotsu Tsukemen.

**Per serving (1 of 8):** 1469 kcal, 66.8g protein, 77.6g fat, 122.9g carbs, 6214mg sodium

High calorie count is expected: 125g raw pork belly/serving + 125g raw flour/serving (thick tsukemen noodles) + rich soy-mirin tare.

**Extraction modeling (AC#3):**
- Pork bones/chicken feet/chicken backs: Extraction estimates (15-25 kcal/100g) — solids discarded
- Pork belly: Full raw values (518 kcal/100g) — fat renders into consumed broth, meat eaten as chashu
- Dried fish weighted by breakdown: niboshi (89% strained → 110 kcal/100g), katsuobushi (78% strained → 140 kcal/100g), sababushi (68% strained → 162 kcal/100g), dried flounder powder (100% consumed → 330 kcal/100g)

**Per-bowl assembly (AC#4):** Total batch ÷ 8 servings = per-bowl. All ingredients in PREP gather section represent full batch.

**Soup-wari (AC#5):** Already included in totals — broth_water (4000g) covers both assembly and soup-wari portions.
