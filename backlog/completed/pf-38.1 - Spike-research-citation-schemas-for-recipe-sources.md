## Final Summary

<!-- SECTION:FINAL_SUMMARY:BEGIN -->
Spike complete. Researched 3 strategies in parallel:\n\n1. **Schema standards**: schema.org `isBasedOn` is the semantic match for recipe adaptation. Key properties: author, isBasedOn, citation, creditText.\n2. **Real-world patterns**: Paprika uses `source` + `source_url`. NYT/food blogs use prose \"adapted from\". Two fields (name + URL) is universal minimum.\n3. **Codebase analysis**: meta.source is plain string across 4 recipes. next_time is string[] (1 recipe, 5 entries). 2 consumer sites. No normalization layer.\n\nProposed schema documented in PF-38 implementation notes. Risk analysis spawned PF-38.3 through PF-38.6.
