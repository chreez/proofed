Tradeoffs

Best for
Discovery-first workflows; users browsing recipes and incrementally building a queue. Lower interaction cost than drag.
Worst for
Reordering the queue (no drag in cart). Sidebar competes with cards for screen real estate on narrower laptops.
Mobile fallback
Grid hides entirely; cart becomes the only content as a review-mode list. Adding new bakes happens from recipe pages.
