PF-196 Demo: Reveal Mechanism Options

The index defaults to showing only baked recipes (those with cook_log entries). Unbaked recipes are hidden. These three options show different ways to reveal them. In-progress bakes (with scratchpad data) float to the top in all options.

A monospace text link below the last category. Click to reveal unbaked recipes inline (dimmed) within their categories. Minimal, unobtrusive.
