    Tradeoffs

    Best for
    Power users building a queue from a known shortlist; high-density compose sessions where library + queue need parallel scanning.
    Worst for
    Touch-only users; first-time users who don't know what a queue is yet. Drag affordance has a learning curve.
    Mobile fallback
    Library column hides; queue becomes the entire screen as a read-only review list. Add/edit moves to recipe pages.
